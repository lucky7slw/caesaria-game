# CaesarIA Premium - Asset Requirements

## Overview
This document outlines all assets required for the comprehensive premium transformation of CaesarIA for iOS/iPadOS.

## 3D Models & Architecture

### Buildings (100+ models needed)
- **Monuments**: Colosseum, Pantheon, Circus Maximus, Baths of Caracalla, Forum of Trajan
- **Religious**: Temples to 12 major gods, mystery cult temples (Mithraeum), early Christian catacombs
- **Infrastructure**: Aqueducts (multiple tiers), bridges, roads, harbors, lighthouses
- **Housing**: Insulae (apartment buildings), domus (townhouses), villas (country estates)
- **Commercial**: Markets, warehouses, shops, workshops, thermopolia (snack bars)
- **Military**: Barracks, walls, towers, gates, armories
- **Entertainment**: Theaters, amphitheaters, circuses, bath complexes
- **Civic**: Senate house, basilicas, administrative buildings
- **Industrial**: Olive presses, wine production, pottery kilns, blacksmiths

### Citizens & Characters (50+ character models)
- **Age variations**: Children, young adults, middle-aged, elderly
- **Social classes**: Patricians (nobles), plebeians (commoners), slaves, freedmen
- **Occupations**: Soldiers, merchants, farmers, craftsmen, priests, senators, gladiators, charioteers
- **Animations**: Walking, working, talking, celebrating, rioting, fleeing, fighting, dining

### Vehicles & Objects
- **Transport**: Carts, wagons, litters (sedan chairs), ships (merchant, military, grain ships)
- **Military**: Siege weapons (ballistas, catapults, battering rams), shields, gladius swords, pila (javelins)
- **Daily objects**: Amphorae (storage jars), furniture, tools, food items, scrolls, tablets

### Nature & Environment
- **Vegetation**: Olive trees, grape vines, cypress trees, palms, gardens, crops (wheat, vegetables)
- **Terrain**: Hills, mountains, rivers (Tiber), coastlines, Mediterranean landscapes
- **Wildlife**: Horses, cattle, sheep, dogs, exotic animals for games (lions, elephants)

## Acquisition Strategy

### Sources

1. **Create Original Assets**
   - Commission 3D artists specializing in historical reconstruction
   - Partner with historical consultants for accuracy
   - Develop in-house using Blender, Maya, Substance Painter

2. **License from Asset Stores**
   - Unreal Marketplace
   - Unity Asset Store
   - TurboSquid, CGTrader
   - Sketchfab (many Roman assets available)
   - Quixel Megascans (high-quality textures and materials)

3. **Museum Partnerships**
   - British Museum (Open collection license for many artifacts)
   - Smithsonian (extensive 3D scan collection)
   - Louvre
   - Vatican Museums
   - Museums in Rome (Capitoline, National Roman Museum)

4. **Academic/Archaeological**
   - Partner with universities (classics departments)
   - Archaeological Institute of America
   - Digital reconstructions from archaeological projects
   - Photogrammetry from excavation sites

5. **Open Source/Creative Commons**
   - Sketchfab (CC-licensed models)
   - Wikimedia Commons (textures, historical images)
   - Open Game Art
   - NASA (for space mod - planets, stars)

6. **Audio**
   - Epidemic Sound (royalty-free music library)
   - AudioJungle
   - Freesound.org (CC-licensed sound effects)
   - Commission composers for original Roman-inspired music
   - Record custom foley and sound effects

7. **AI Generation** (Supplement, not replace)
   - Midjourney, Stable Diffusion for texture generation
   - AI upscaling for historical photographs
   - AI voice generation for citizens (Apple Neural Engine TTS)

### Budget Considerations

**Estimated Asset Budget:**
- **3D Models**: $50k-100k (mix of commissioned and licensed)
- **Textures/Materials**: $10k-20k (mostly licensed, some commissioned)
- **Audio**: $20k-40k (music commission + SFX libraries)
- **Historical Images/Video**: $5k-10k (licensing fees)
- **Voice Acting** (if not fully AI): $10k-30k
- **Total Estimated**: $95k-200k

**Cost-Saving Strategies:**
- Use AI voice generation for most dialogue (Apple Neural TTS - free)
- Leverage open-source and CC-licensed content where possible
- Partner with universities for historical accuracy review in exchange for credit
- Museum partnerships may provide assets in exchange for educational promotion
- Modular assets (reuse with variations)
- Procedural generation for some elements (mosaic patterns, graffiti)

## Asset Management

### Organization
- Clear folder structure by type (models/buildings/, models/characters/, textures/, audio/)
- Consistent naming conventions
- LOD (Level of Detail) versions for performance (high, medium, low poly)
- Asset database/tracking spreadsheet

### Formats
- **3D Models**: FBX, OBJ (for interchange), native format for game engine
- **Textures**: PNG (lossless), compressed for iOS (ASTC, PVRTC)
- **Audio**: AAC, MP3 (compressed for mobile), WAV (for sound effects)
- **Video**: H.264, HEVC for iOS playback

### Optimization for iOS
- Mobile-optimized poly counts (<50k tris for main buildings, <10k for props)
- Texture atlasing to reduce draw calls
- Compressed texture formats (ASTC for iOS)
- Audio compression (AAC at appropriate bitrates)
- LOD system for distant objects
- Occlusion culling

## Legal & Rights

- **Licensing**: Ensure all assets properly licensed for commercial use
- **Attribution**: Track attribution requirements for CC and museum assets
- **Copyright**: Verify no copyright issues with historical reconstructions
- **Trademark**: Avoid trademarked content (e.g., specific modern interpretations)
- **Cultural Sensitivity**: Review with historians for respectful portrayal

## Quality Standards

- **Historical Accuracy**: All realistic assets reviewed by historical consultants
- **Visual Consistency**: Cohesive art style across all assets
- **Performance**: Optimized for iOS devices (iPhone 12+ target)
- **Accessibility**: Consider color-blind modes, high-contrast options
- **Localization**: UI text translatable, avoid text in textures
