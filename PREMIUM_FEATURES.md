## 🌟 Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an internet connection?

**No!** CaesarIA works completely offline after installation. Internet is only needed for:
- Multiplayer features
- Workshop downloads
- iCloud sync
- Live Activities

### Does this drain my battery?

Strategy games use more power than simple apps. Expect:
- **iPhone Pro (120Hz)**: 2-3 hours
- **iPhone standard**: 4-5 hours
- **Battery Saver mode**: 6-8 hours
- **iPad Pro**: 5-7 hours

### Can I use Apple Pencil 1?

**Yes!** Apple Pencil 1 is fully supported with precision, pressure sensitivity, and tilt. Advanced features (Squeeze, Barrel Roll, Hover, Haptics) require **Apple Pencil Pro** with compatible iPad.

### Is ray tracing available on my device?

**Ray tracing requires**:
- iPhone 15 Pro or iPhone 15 Pro Max
- iPad Pro with M1, M2, or M3 chip
- Metal 3.1 support

Older devices use high-quality rasterization with screen-space reflections and shadow maps.

### How much does it cost?

**CaesarIA is open-source and free!** You only need:
1. **Caesar III resource files** (~$6 on Steam/GOG, often on sale for $2)
2. **Mac with Xcode** (to build and install on your iOS device)

### Can I play my saves across devices?

**Yes!** With iCloud sync enabled, all your saves, settings, and custom content automatically sync across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

### Does this work with game controllers?

**Yes!** Fully supports:
- Xbox Wireless Controller
- PlayStation DualSense
- MFi (Made for iPhone) controllers
- Keyboard + Mouse (on iPad)

All controls are fully remappable.

### Can I mod the game on iOS?

**Yes!** The full modding API is available on iOS. You can:
- Create mods using Lua or JavaScript
- Use the in-game level editor
- Download mods from the Workshop
- Share your creations

**Note**: Creating complex mods is easier on iPad with keyboard or with a Mac for the coding parts.

### Is multiplayer cross-platform?

**Yes!** Play with friends on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows, Linux, and Android. All platforms can play together.

### Does this support family sharing?

**Yes!** One purchase (of Caesar III), multiple family members can build and install CaesarIA on their own devices. Save games can optionally be shared within the family.
